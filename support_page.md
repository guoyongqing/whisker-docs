# Whisker Support

Need help? Most answers are below. If you don't see yours, email us at shadeless@126.com.

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: I forgot to back up before reinstalling. Can I recover my data?**
No. By design, Whisker has no server and no copy of your records. The encrypted database lives only on your device. Always create a backup before deleting the app or moving to a new phone — Settings → Backup.

**Q: How does the 12-word backup work?**
We generate a BIP39 mnemonic phrase (12 English words). The phrase derives an AES-256 key that encrypts your backup file. To restore, enter the same 12 words on a new device. Write them down on paper and store them somewhere safe. We cannot recover them for you.

**Q: Why is my Apple Health step count not showing up?**
1. Open Settings → Whisker → make sure Apple Health permission is granted.
2. Open the iOS Health app → Sharing → Apps → Whisker → make sure "Steps" and "Walking + Running Distance" are both turned on.
3. If you wear an Apple Watch, give the Health app a few minutes to sync after a walk.

**Q: I scanned a lab report but no values were extracted.**
- Make sure the document is in good lighting and the text is sharp.
- Try the AI extraction (Premium): on iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence-capable devices, Foundation Models can read messy or handwritten reports.
- If extraction still misses fields, you can always type them in manually — they'll be saved with the same chart and trend tracking.

**Q: I subscribed but Premium features are still locked.**
1. Open the Paywall screen and tap "Restore Purchases".
2. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to purchase.
3. If still stuck, force-quit and reopen Whisker.

**Q: How do I cancel my subscription?**
On iOS: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Whisker → Cancel. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period.

**Q: Does Whisker work without internet?**
Yes. The only feature that needs the network is the in-app purchase flow, and even that can fall back to "Restore Purchases" later.
